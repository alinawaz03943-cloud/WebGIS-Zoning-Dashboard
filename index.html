        About this map

        A public reference map of zoning districts within the City of Danville, Virginia. Hover over a district to see its code; select it for all attributes in the dataset, plus any overlays that cover that spot.

        This is an informational mapping tool. It does not identify the zoning of a specific property or parcel. Confirm official zoning determinations with the City of Danville.
